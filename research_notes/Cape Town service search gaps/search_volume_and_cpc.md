## What are monthly search volumes for "[service] cape town" / "[service] near me" keywords in South Africa/Cape Town?

### Takeaway
No measured keyword search volumes for any "[service] cape town" or SA "[service] near me" keyword could be obtained: public pages that publish keyword volumes are almost all US data, and keyword tools were unreachable. The only SA demand data found is marketplace request data (Procompare 2024, Kandua 2022), which is a demand proxy, not Google search volume.

### Cited Findings
- Four separate searches for Cape Town / SA keyword volumes (plumber, electrician, locksmith, pest control, tree felling, rubble removal, cleaning services) returned no SA figures; the keyword tables that surfaced were US data, e.g. "plumber near me" 823,000 searches at $18.98 CPC (US) — [Causal Funnel](https://www.causalfunnel.com/blog/top-100-plumbing-keywords-to-boost-your-business/); "locksmith near me" 204,000 searches, $3.17 CPC (USD, country not stated, likely US) — [Loopex Digital](https://www.loopexdigital.com/blog/plumbing-keywords-seo-ppc); US "plumber" ~550,000/mo and "emergency plumber" ~60,500/mo — [plumberseo.net](https://www.plumberseo.net/seo-strategy-for-plumbers-plumbing-contractors/most-commonly-searched-plumbing-keywords/). **These are not usable for Cape Town.**
- UK reference point: "plumber" 8,100 searches/month with a top-of-page bid around £5; "emergency plumber" variant £9.52-£22 per click — [One Base Media](https://onebasemedia.co.uk/how-much-do-google-ads-cost) (UK, undated in snippet).
- SA demand proxy (not search volume): Procompare analysed 100,000+ service requests submitted January-December 2024; top five categories by demand were plumbers, building contractors, cleaning services, solar panels, accountants — [Procompare](https://www.procompare.co.za/pro-info-centre/trends/services-in-demand). National, no Western Cape breakdown.
- Same Procompare analysis, year-on-year growth in requests: air conditioning +156%, plumbers +73%, building contractors +23%, electricians +21% — [Procompare](https://www.procompare.co.za/pro-info-centre/trends/services-in-demand).
- Procompare seasonality (as reported in the snippet; column meaning inferred as low month / peak month / peak-to-trough ratio): plumbers January/May/1.8; building contractors November/January/2.0; cleaning services March/July/3.5; solar panels January/August/1.7; accountants 3.9 — [Procompare](https://www.procompare.co.za/pro-info-centre/trends/services-in-demand).
- SA local-SEO guides recommend "[trade] + [suburb]" and "near me" structures (e.g. "Cape Town Southern Suburbs" as a plumber modifier) but publish no volumes — [Moonlit](https://www.moonlit.co.za/blog/local-seo-for-contractors-and-home-services-south-africa/).

## What are typical Google Ads CPCs (top-of-page bid low/high range) for those keywords in South Africa? Which categories are cheapest and most expensive?

### Takeaway
No keyword-level top-of-page bid ranges were found; only agency planning bands and category averages. Home-services/emergency trades are consistently quoted at roughly R12-R65 per click (plumber about R20-R30 in Cape Town), legal and insurance are the most expensive (R45-R150+), and the cheapest measured category averages sit around R4-R18.

### Cited Findings
- City-level estimate: a "plumber" keyword costs R30-R45 in Johannesburg, R20-R30 in Cape Town, R12-R18 in Bloemfontein; described by the agency as rough estimates from real accounts — [New Age Marketing](https://newagemarketing.co.za/how-google-ads-pricing-works/).
- Home services (plumbing, electrical): R15-R40; legal R80-R150+; insurance R80-R130 — [Scope](https://scope.co.za/blog/google-ads/costs/) (2026 guide).
- Emergency trades (plumbers, electricians) R25-R65; local emergency services (plumbing, electricians, locksmiths) R25-R55; solar and backup inverter systems R35-R78; legal and attorneys R45-R95; retail and e-commerce R8-R22 — [Moonlit](https://www.moonlit.co.za/blog/google-ads-cost-south-africa-pricing-guide/) (2026 guide).
- Home services (plumbing, electrical) R12-R30; legal R45-R120; emergency terms are the most expensive — [Nines Media](https://www.ninesmedia.com/blog/google-ads-cost-south-africa.html).
- "Home & Local Services (plumbers, installers, medical): R15-R100 CPC depending on suburb and urgency" — one of the 2026 SA agency guides returned in the same search; the snippet did not attribute it to a specific page — candidates: [Searchly](https://searchly.co.za/blog/google-ads/google-ads-costs/), [DMA101](https://dma101.com/blog/google-ads-cost-south-africa-2026), [Signal Growth Partners](https://signalgrowthpartners.co.za/how-much-does-google-ads-cost/). Attribution unverified.
- Home Services R10-R30 (2025-dated guide) — attributed by the search summary to [Active Advertising](https://activeadvertising.co.za/how-much-should-you-spend-on-google-ads-a-practical-guide-for-south-african-small-businesses-in-2025/) (attribution not page-verified).
- Local services CPCs "often quoted at R5-R15"; Cape Town high-competition categories R25-R50 or more — [Aion Marketing](https://aionmarketing.co.za/how-to-reduce-google-ads-cost-per-click-in-south-africa/) and [Growth Pulse Media, Cape Town](https://www.growthpulsemedia.co.za/google-ads-cost-cape-town/) (the summary did not cleanly separate which figure belongs to which page).
- Cleaning services: an agency states precise cleaning CPC data is not in any public benchmark report; it derives a planning cost per lead of about R116 from CPC R12.42 and a 10.75% conversion rate (SA "Consumer Services" benchmark). Single-city commercial cleaning campaigns typically need R10,000-R20,000/month — [Growth Pulse Media, cleaning](https://www.growthpulsemedia.co.za/google-ads-for-cleaning-services-south-africa/). This is a calculation, not a measured result.
- Good local-service cost per lead: R150-R600 — [Growth Pulse Media, Cape Town](https://www.growthpulsemedia.co.za/google-ads-cost-cape-town/).
- "R12-R20 sits in the strong range for most ecommerce and home services accounts" (rule of thumb) — [Growth Pulse Media](https://www.growthpulsemedia.co.za/google-ads-cpc-ctr-benchmarks-south-africa/).
- Suggested starting budget for local service businesses: R5,000-R8,000/month ad spend; management fees R2,500-R7,500/month — SA agency guides incl. [Moonlit](https://www.moonlit.co.za/blog/google-ads-cost-south-africa-pricing-guide/) (exact page attribution per figure not verified).
- Anecdotal: SA CPC "generally falls between R10 and R150 depending on industry and competition" (forum post, August 2025) — [Google product forum](https://groups.google.com/g/googleproductforums/c/bM-KK1WLV5I).

### Inferences
- Most expensive (well supported across sources): insurance, legal, then finance; within services, emergency trades (locksmith, emergency plumber/electrician, blocked drains, geyser) and solar.
- Likely cheapest among local services: non-emergency, low-ticket, low-urgency categories (cleaning at about R12, general "consumer services", possibly garden services, dog grooming, laundry, car wash). Only cleaning has any number behind it; the rest is inference from the pattern that urgency and ticket size drive CPC.
- "Under R10/click" is plausible for some local categories per the "R5-R15" quote and AdBot's low averages, but no source names a specific trade keyword under R10.

### Gaps
- No Keyword Planner top-of-page low/high bids for any keyword.
- No SA CPC found for: pest control, rubble removal, tree felling, garden services, pool cleaning, appliance repair, aircon, gate/garage motors, movers, car wash/valet, towing, borehole, CCTV/alarms, electric fencing, glazing, laundry, skip hire, septic, dog grooming, physiotherapy.
- All bands come from agencies that sell Google Ads management; none states sample size for these bands.

## What are overall Google Ads CPC benchmarks in South Africa by industry, and how do they compare to US/UK?

### Takeaway
Published SA benchmarks disagree by more than an order of magnitude (overall averages from about R4-R6 up to R88.97), so only relative ordering is trustworthy. Even so, SA home-services clicks (roughly R12-R40) cost a fraction of the US home-services average of $7.85 per click.

### Cited Findings
- BaseCloud (SA agency's own account data; sample and date range not stated; page roughly 10 months old at time of search) average search CPC: Construction & Trades R13.27 (CTR 8.42%); Auto Repairs & Services R17.44 (CTR 11.60%); Consumer Services R12.42 (CTR 10.00%); Law & Mediation R14.58; Renewable Energy R19.96; Health & Medical CTR 18.04% — [BaseCloud](https://basecloudglobal.com/google-ads-benchmarks-for-south-african-industries/).
- AdBot 2025 (based on 1,200+ local Google search campaigns): overall SA average R4-R6 per click; Financial Services R4.00; Health Services R3.90 (CTR 10.4%); Other Services R6.00; Manufacturing R6.70; Professional Services R6.30 — [AdBot](https://adbot.co.za/how-to-advertise-online/google-ads-benchmarks-for-your-sa-industry-2025/).
- Older AdBot benchmark (publication date not visible; a companion article is dated May 2022, so treat as about 2022): artist and photography R3.63; automotive service and repair R7.70; SA CTRs are several per cent higher than international in almost all industries — [Bizcommunity](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/first-sa-google-search-cpc-and-ctr-industry-benchmark-626811a); [The Media Online, 2022](https://themediaonline.co.za/2022/05/local-businesses-need-local-insights-to-maximise-google-ad-spend).
- Juicy Designs 2026: 1,224 SA campaigns on Google Ads and Microsoft Ads, July 2025-June 2026, 24 industries; average CPC R88.97, CTR 6.64%, CVR 8.18%, CPL R1,294.70; lowest Arts and Entertainment R30.36, highest Insurance R195.52 — [Juicy Designs benchmark](https://www.juicydesigns.co.za/research/south-african-search-advertising-benchmarks-2026/); [Juicy Designs agency guide](https://www.juicydesigns.co.za/blog/digital-marketing-agency-guide/). Per-industry rows not retrieved.
- Growth Pulse Media (Pretoria guide) search CPCs: Legal R103, Technology R58, Finance R53, Travel R24, E-commerce R18 — [Growth Pulse Media](https://www.growthpulsemedia.co.za/google-ads-cost-pretoria/). (Page titles carry "2026/2027 guide" labels that look like SEO date-stuffing; treat dates with caution.)
- Other overall averages quoted: about R9-R15 across industries; about R18-R28 across most industries; R10-R30 — SA agency guides returned together ([Scope](https://scope.co.za/blog/google-ads/costs/), [Searchly](https://searchly.co.za/blog/google-ads/google-ads-costs/), [Signal Growth Partners](https://signalgrowthpartners.co.za/how-much-does-google-ads-cost/)); per-figure attribution not verified.
- AgencyAnalytics: SA average CPC $0.28, based on 51,567 data points, as of February 2025 — [AgencyAnalytics](https://agencyanalytics.com/blog/google-ads-cost).
- Statista: average Google Ads search CPC in South Africa was 51 US cents in May 2023 (older than preferred window) — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1262499/search-advertising-cpc-africa/).
- US comparison: home services average search CPC $7.85, CTR 6.37%, cost per lead $90.92; sample 3,211 US campaigns, 1 April 2024-21 March 2025; CPC rose for 75% of home-services businesses — [LocaliQ](https://localiq.com/blog/home-services-search-advertising-benchmarks/). All-industry US average $5.42 (WordStream-based 2026 figure) — [Focus Digital](https://focus-digital.co/average-google-ads-cost-per-click-by-industry/). Another table lists Home Services $6.55 CPC, 5.31% CTR — [Conversion Studio](https://conversion.studio/blog/google-ads-benchmarks).
- US keyword level: "plumber near me" $18-$32 average CPC — [Webtonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/plumbing-google-ads-statistics).
- UK comparison: plumbing £3-£10 per click, electrical £2-£8 — [Trade2Base](https://www.trade2base.com/blog/google-ads-trade-business-uk).
- Australia comparison: plumber about $25, electrician about $22 average CPC — [Sora Solutions](https://sora-solutions.vercel.app/blog/how-much-tradies-spend-google-ads).

### Inferences
- The R4-R6 (AdBot) versus R88.97 (Juicy Designs) gap is too large to be noise; likely causes are client mix (AdBot serves micro-budget SMEs, Juicy Designs probably larger B2B/insurance accounts), inclusion of Microsoft Ads, and mean versus median. Neither should be used as "the" SA average. One search summary described the Juicy Designs study as "Adbot-linked"; that link is unverified and looks like a search-summary error.
- For local trades, BaseCloud's R12-R18 category averages and the agency bands of R12-R40 are mutually consistent and are the most defensible planning range.
- Rough conversion only (exchange rate not sourced in this research; assume order of R17-R19 per USD): US home services $7.85 is about R135-R150 per click, i.e. roughly 4-10 times SA home-services CPCs. UK plumbing £3-£10 is likewise several times the Cape Town R20-R30 estimate.

### Gaps
- Full industry tables from BaseCloud, AdBot, Juicy Designs and Statista's "Google Ads CPC by industry in South Africa 2023" could not be read.
- No SA benchmark has rows for cleaning, security, pest control or moving specifically.

## Which categories show the best volume-to-CPC ratio?

### Takeaway
A numeric volume/CPC ratio cannot be computed because no SA search volumes were found. The only defensible ranking is a qualitative one combining Procompare demand rank with CPC bands, which points to cleaning services and aircon as the most promising "high demand, comparatively low CPC" candidates and flags plumbing/locksmith/solar as high demand but expensive.

### Cited Findings
- Demand side: plumbers #1, cleaning services #3, solar #4 by 2024 request volume; aircon +156% and plumbers +73% YoY — [Procompare](https://www.procompare.co.za/pro-info-centre/trends/services-in-demand).
- Cost side: cleaning proxied at R12.42 CPC and about R116 planning CPL — [Growth Pulse Media](https://www.growthpulsemedia.co.za/google-ads-for-cleaning-services-south-africa/); plumbing/electrical R12-R65 with emergency terms highest — [Moonlit](https://www.moonlit.co.za/blog/google-ads-cost-south-africa-pricing-guide/), [Nines Media](https://www.ninesmedia.com/blog/google-ads-cost-south-africa.html); solar R35-R78 — [Moonlit](https://www.moonlit.co.za/blog/google-ads-cost-south-africa-pricing-guide/); construction & trades R13.27 — [BaseCloud](https://basecloudglobal.com/google-ads-benchmarks-for-south-african-industries/).

### Inferences
- Qualitative ordering (inference, not measurement):
  1. Cleaning services: top-3 demand, lowest cited CPC (about R12). Best supported candidate.
  2. Aircon installation/repair: fastest-growing demand; no CPC found, so unconfirmed.
  3. General building/trades (painting, paving, tiling, roofing, handyman): #2 demand as "building contractors", category CPC about R13.
  4. Plumbing / electrical: highest demand but R20-R65 clicks; ratio dragged down by emergency-term pricing. Non-emergency variants may be better.
  5. Solar: high demand but R35-R78 and demand tied to load-shedding cycles.
  6. Locksmith: priced in the top emergency band with no demand data.
- Long-tail categories with no data (rubble removal, tree felling, garden services, dog grooming, laundry, septic, skip hire, car wash) are the natural places to look for low-CPC gaps, but that is a hypothesis to test with Keyword Planner, not a finding.

### Gaps
- No ratio can be computed. Required input: Keyword Planner export for about 40 "[service] cape town" keywords with average monthly searches and top-of-page low/high bids, location set to Cape Town.
